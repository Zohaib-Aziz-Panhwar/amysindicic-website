Before you book

I'm still in government. Is it too early to start?

No — it's the best time. Positioning takes weeks, not days, and the leaders who land well start well before their target exit. Direction work is completely confidential.

Yes. Retiring officers and senior NCOs, federal civilians at GS-13 through SES, state and local government leaders, and contractors moving into industry leadership are the core of Amy's practice.

Is everything done with Amy personally?

Every session, résumé and profile is written and coached by Amy — never handed to an assistant or generated by a template.

How do sessions run?

Sessions run online at times that fit your schedule. Documents are delivered in editable Word and PDF formats.

Do you also write SES and federal executive packages?

Yes — Amy writes SES résumés, ECQ and TQ narratives for leaders advancing within government. Free samples are in the resource library.
